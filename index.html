🎮 How to Play

Combine matching numbers to reach 2048 and beat your best score!

2+2→4

How to play

  1. Swipe up, down, left or right to move all tiles.
  2. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one bigger tile.
  3. Every successful move adds a new 2 or 4 tile.
  4. Keep merging to create 2048!

Game rules

  • Only matching numbers merge: 2+2=4, 4+4=8, 8+8=16, and so on.
  • Each tile can merge only once per move.
  • A move that changes nothing does not add a tile.
  • You earn points equal to the value of each new merged tile.
  • The game ends when the board is full and no matching neighbors remain.
  • Reach 2048 to win, then choose to keep playing for a higher score.

💡 Tip: Keep your biggest number in a corner and build around it. Use Undo to reverse your last move.
